**Miami Crime 6** is an original open-world crime game set on the sun-soaked, neon-lit **Solara Coast**. Play as dual protagonists **Marco Vega** (ex-getaway driver) and **Elena Cruz** (heist tactician) across six seamless districts, from Art Deco beachfront boulevards to alligator-infested wetlands.

## Highlights

- **True 3D WebGL Engine (Three.js)**: Full third-person 3D open world with over-the-shoulder on-foot camera, dynamic speed-reactive vehicle chase camera, procedural Art Deco skyscrapers, animated turquoise ocean, and dynamic Golden Hour, Tropical Day, and Neon Night skies.
- **6 Interconnected 3D Districts**: Bayside Strip, Coral Keys, Cypress Marsh, Harbor Point, Solara Flats, and Pineridge Overlook — seamlessly explorable by car, motorcycle, speedboat, or on foot.
- **Dual Protagonists (`Tab`)**: Switch live between Marco Vega and Elena Cruz anytime; your partner AI follows, rides passenger, and lays down covering fire in gunfights.
- **Vehicles, Boats & Auto Mod Garage (`F` / `E`)**: Steal and drive Neon Coupes, Muscle Cars, Motorcycles, SUVs, Taxis, SC-PD Interceptors, SWAT Vans, and KeyRunner Speedboats. Repaint cars, bolt on Twin-Turbo upgrades, and swap `HOT PLATE` tags to shake police heat.
- **1–5 Star Wanted System & Living World**: Escalating SC-PD pursuit cruisers, on-foot officers, SWAT vans, and a sweeping 4–5 star Helicopter Searchlight — plus pedestrians with daily routines & NPC witness memory (fleeing or filming you on phones) and patrolling Cypress Marsh alligators.
- **6-Mission Campaign + Hidden Mysteries**: Pull off *Motel Stickup*, *Marina Hotwire*, *Marsh Cargo*, *Harbor Ambush*, *Club Mirage Intel*, and *Bayside Vault Heist*, or uncover the Swamp UFO Anomaly, Coral Reef Shipwreck, and Ghost Radio Beacon.
- **SOLAGRAM Viral Feed & Procedural Synthwave Radio (`M`)**: Live viral social posts react to your drifts, chases, gator encounters, and heists, backed by a 100% Web Audio API synthwave & coastline chill radio.
- **Offline Static Build & Save/Load (`Esc`)**: Runs completely offline in desktop Chrome with zero external network requests and persists full progress to `localStorage`.

## Controls & Features

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `W A S D` / `Arrows` | Move on foot / Steer & drive |
| `Mouse` + `Left Click` | Aim & fire weapon (or melee) |
| `1`–`5` / `R` | Select weapon / Reload |
| `F` | Steal, enter, or exit vehicle or speedboat |
| `Space` / `Shift` | Handbrake drift / Nitro boost |
| `Tab` | Switch live between Marco Vega & Elena Cruz |
| `E` | Enter Safehouse, Auto Mod Garage, Bank, Club, or Boutique |
| `M` / `T` | Cycle Synthwave Radio / Cycle Time of Day |
| `Esc` | Pause, Save & Load menu |

**Quick Feature Summary:** Third-person Three.js WebGL open world across 6 districts · live dual-protagonist switching with AI partner combat · cars, motorcycles, police interceptors, SWAT vans & speedboats · Auto Mod Garage (repaints, twin-turbo, `HOT PLATE` swaps) · 1–5 star wanted pursuits with helicopter searchlight · 6-mission campaign + 3 hidden mysteries · SOLAGRAM viral feed & procedural Web Audio synthwave radio · full offline `localStorage` save/load.

## Run Locally

```bash
node serve.ts
```

Then open `http://localhost:8080` in Chrome.
